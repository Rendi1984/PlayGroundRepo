Tides of Faith

An original god game

You are a young god on an island of seven villages. A rival god, the Tidewraith, competes for the hearts of mortals. Every village that believes in you prays — and the more believers you have, the more mana flows to you. Miracles can only be cast inside your influence.

  • ✋ Hand of Light: lift villagers and trees and throw them. Set a villager down gently in another village and they move in; a hard throw hurts — and frightens.
  • ☄️ Fireball: press inside your influence, drag to any target and release — strike villages far beyond your borders.
  • Miracles: Rain, Bounty, Heal, Fertility (more babies) and Joy (a festival) inspire love. Fire, Lightning and Fireball inspire fear.
  • 🔨 Build: four house styles, granary, well, garden and shrine — and found new settlements. Villagers haul the wood and build it themselves.
  • Happiness & children: happy, well-fed villages have children; children grow up; more people means more mana and wider influence.
  • Ember, your Phoenix, learns from what you do — and its form follows your soul: Benevolent Guardian, Awakening, or Harbinger of Destruction. Two advisors, Aurel and Cinder, whisper opposite counsel.
  • Win: every village believes in you. Lose: none does.
