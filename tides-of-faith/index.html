Tides of Faith

An original god game

You are a young god with a single village on a wide island. Far across it, a rival god, the Tidewraith, has a village of its own — and both of you will spread by founding new settlements. Every village that believes in you prays — and the more believers you have, the more mana flows to you. Miracles can only be cast inside your influence.

  • ✋ Hand of Light: lift villagers and trees and throw them. Set a villager down gently in another village and they move in; a hard throw hurts — and frightens.
  • ☄️ Fireball: press inside your influence to conjure it, then fling it with the mouse — a hard throw reaches villages far beyond your borders. 🛡 Shield protects a village from fire, lightning and fireballs.
  • Miracles: Rain (it also makes forests grow), Bounty, Heal, Fertility (more babies) and Joy (a festival) inspire love. Fire, Lightning and Fireball inspire fear.
  • ⛰ Shape Land: raise hills and mountains, dig ponds and lakes, flatten ground, grow forests.
  • 🔨 Build: four house styles, granary, well, garden and shrine — and found new settlements. Villagers haul the wood and build it themselves.
  • ⚔ War: build a Barracks to train soldiers, then send your army against a village — defeat its defenders and hold its plaza to conquer it. The Tidewraith raises armies too.
  • Wildlife & migration: villagers hunt deer and boar for meat; people move to whichever god's village offers the better life.
  • Happiness & children: happy, well-fed villages have children; children grow up; more people means more mana and wider influence.
  • Ember, your Phoenix, learns from what you do — and its form follows your soul: Benevolent Guardian, Awakening, or Harbinger of Destruction. Two advisors, Aurel and Cinder, whisper opposite counsel.
  • Win: the Tidewraith has no village left, or 70% of all villages believe in you. Lose: no village believes in you for 40 seconds.
